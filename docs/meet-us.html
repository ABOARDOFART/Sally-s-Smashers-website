Bob Barley

Bob Barley is the owner and lead blacksmith of the company. He spends most of his day at the forge working away on the next batch of weapons. He's happy to answer questions at tours and give demonstrations.

John Doe

John Doe, previous deer breeder, is a guide for the company. He has a degree in history so if you want to know about the ancient techniques used he will be the bets person to ask.

Jane Jammy

Jane Jammy is currently Bob Barley's apprentice and shadow who is learning how to make the best quality weapons. She also acts as a guide and gives insightful and personal knowledge about the process of metalworking.

Milo Herrell

Milo Herrell is the website designer for Sally's Smashers. He also handles the weapon orders and tour bookings.
