Why come?

Visiting the forges at Sally's is a once in a lifetime opportunity to see the flames of the forge up close. You've no doubt seen medieval swords and tools before, now is your chance to see how it's all made. We will even give you the chance to try your hand at working with the metal.

This experience is also educational, you'll see how metal has been worked on historically and how it's been updated for the modern world, we will talk about the materials used and the design elements put into swords. We also include a section criticizing fantasy swords and how they could be improved to be more effective or realistic during medieval times.

What will you see?

When you come to the forge, you will be able to see a live demonstration of how we make swords and even get a chance to participate for a small part.

You can try your hand at making arrowheads, daggers or throwing knives.

How can you come?

The tours happen start at 10:00 AM from monday to friday, lunch will be offered. The prices and tour guides are displayed below.
