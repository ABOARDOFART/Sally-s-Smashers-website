Who We Are

Sally's Smashers offers great quality swords, maces and other medieval weaponry. Our storefront is located in Montreal with deliveries all across Canada. Our forge, however, is located in Ottowa. Since 1997 we've made quality swords and even offered a chance at ordering custom ones and offering tours of the forge. We are excited to have you see what we can offer you.

Custom Swords

At Sally's we offer the unique chance for the customer to order a sword of their own design. To order this sword you will need to know how to properly describe how your sword will look like.

Our Tours

You can find an opportunity to take a visit of our beautiful forges in Ottowa. We offer guided tours and an immersive experience to allow you to understand how we build our beautiful weapons.
